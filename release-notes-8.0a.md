## 8.0a — 2026-10-17 — the conductor

- **Macros.** A tab of its own:
  playlists of the rig's own
  moves, built block by block —
  Scratch for the instrument.
  Thirty-two block kinds cover
  every engine the app presents:
  sends, Serve (standing or
  custom doors), the beacon and
  GATT hosting, Helm buttons,
  War Chests, EMCON and stealth,
  the Banshee, costumes and
  mimicry personas, listening,
  sweeps, knocks, the fuzzer,
  Net-Assault, War Room runs,
  SIGINT watches, War Games
  challenges and defenses — and
  the passive blocks: waits,
  waiting for an arrival or a
  sighting, journal notes,
  announcements, and a stop.
- **Built like blocks.** Each
  block folds open where it
  sits to be configured —
  pickers fed live from the
  book, the chests, the
  personas and the remotes,
  toggles and fields that drop
  down and close back up.
  Blocks drag into order and
  carry a repeat count. A
  macro is dressed by its
  operator: a name, an icon
  and a colour from the
  palette, a note saying what
  it is for. Three templates
  wait in the library; the
  library exports and imports
  as nightjar-macros.json,
  and imports land as copies.
- **Runs honestly.** A run
  card shows the playlist
  before it fires; validation
  names every unready block
  first. Runs leave a live
  log on screen and their
  story in the night log; a
  block that cannot run stops
  the run and says why.
- **Skelli conducts.** She
  builds macros from dictated
  steps — "build a macro
  called Night Post, red with
  a moon: go dark; wait 30;
  sweep" — dresses them from
  the words, adds steps to
  them, runs and stops them,
  lists and deletes them,
  through the same store and
  runner the tab drives. Her
  manual carries the room and
  the whole block catalog.
- **The whetstone rides
  along.** 8.0a also carries
  the entire 7.7.7 quality
  sweep below — folded in
  before it ever wore its
  own tag.

## 7.7.7 — 2026-10-17 — the whetstone

- **The whetstone.** 7.7.7 is
  the quality release: no new
  rooms. The whole tree was
  audited end to end — safety,
  consistency, honesty — and
  sharpened. What follows is
  what the audit found and
  fixed.
- **Races and leaks.** The
  weights download line mutated
  its queue off the main actor
  while the screen appended to
  it; the line is main-actor
  confined now. War Games'
  one-second tick ran for the
  rest of the process after a
  single attach; it runs only
  while held — the room on
  screen, or Skelli mid-match.
- **Honest records.** The
  night log's hash chain
  commits only after the event
  reaches disk — a failed
  write used to leave a
  permanent missing link. A
  failed session-log flush
  keeps its lines for the
  next try. A pairing key
  that will not save to the
  Keychain says so in the
  log. Deliveries lists only
  what reached the disk, and
  its zip can fail out loud.
  The JavaScript workbench no
  longer force-unwraps its
  engine.
- **The census can start.**
  The NFC reader-session
  formats entitlement was
  missing — the tag census
  could not have opened a
  session without it. The
  EMCON audit's five Bonjour
  service types are declared
  now, so those rows can
  answer.
- **One door, every clear.**
  The last bare ShareLink —
  the mouth log's export —
  and the assault report go
  through the ShareItem door
  like everything else. The
  Canary's lessons fold with
  a Show-all; the APDU
  explorer's log is clearable;
  the Helm's remote delete
  confirms first, like every
  other destructive clear.
- **Feel.** Primary actions
  on the quiet surfaces tap
  now: her send button,
  Knocktalk, the Duel, the
  Forge, Deliveries, the
  night log, the Dossier,
  the Helm.
- **Dead weight out.** Eight
  uncalled functions and
  three unused imports
  removed; three stray
  spellings unified with the
  rest of the copy.
