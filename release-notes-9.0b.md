## 9.0b — 2026-10-20 — gamma

- **No crashes.** Two full sweeps
  of the house, trap by trap:
  both parsers depth-capped, the
  force-unwraps and blind
  indexes hunted to none, her
  film crossfade rebuilt so a
  mood change can never pause
  the face on screen. What a
  sweep cannot see, the
  developer flight recorder
  names.
- **The bench keeps its footing.**
  The weights bench once woke a
  four-billion-parameter mind
  in a room too small for it,
  and the system killed the
  whole app — silently,
  mid-answer. Now the load is
  refused when the headroom is
  short, a memory warning
  cancels the generation
  itself, the generation
  watches its own oxygen as it
  thinks, and the cache goes
  back to the system after
  every answer. A smaller mind
  answers instead of no app at
  all.
- **No invented errands.** A
  second reading of an ask now
  dispatches only when the
  operator's own words ground
  it — a bare "tell me more
  about him" stays a
  conversation about him,
  never an unasked sweep of the
  wire.
- **Don't forget.** Told "don't
  forget" something, she keeps
  it like anything she was
  asked to remember — the words
  are a keeping, not a
  clearing.
- **Pictures on a budget.** The
  wallpaper and backdrop films
  now decode against a fixed
  memory budget: a long, large
  film ends early instead of
  taking the app with it.
- **Haptics, rebuilt.** One
  retained, prepared generator
  per beat, and every beat
  spoken on the main thread —
  the feedback that used to go
  missing, the beats fired from
  background work, lands now,
  everywhere it should.
- **Her manners.** A bench's
  scaffolding never reaches the
  transcript: reasoning tags,
  quotation marks, and speaker
  labels are stripped before
  she speaks, her name lives in
  the header only, and a "why"
  on the heels of a refusal
  gets its reason in her own
  voice.
- **She warms up slowly.** Pride
  is a reservoir now, not a
  trophy flash: deeds fill it a
  measure at a time — praise
  included — and no single
  knock or finding crowns her.
  Feelings hold their scene,
  twelve minutes, instead of
  flipping at a threshold, and
  a slow energy under them all
  follows the day: the small
  hours answer slower, a wired
  afternoon quicker.
