## 8.0b — 2026-10-17 — the vanishing

- **The new boot film.**
  The reel is new — 167
  frames, portrait, authored
  to play exactly once — and
  the boot is now a ceremony
  timed to it. The film
  plays through the
  platform's own animated
  player — decoded once, at
  a capped size — the log's
  styles stretch across its
  6.68 seconds, the sphere
  at the centre fills on the
  film's own clock — two
  counter-flowing lattices,
  one rising from the bottom
  of the circle, one falling
  from the top, two arcs
  running the rim against
  each other — and when the
  load completes it
  crystallizes into its final
  ring — and the title
  assembles — letters wide
  and dim drawing together,
  brightening — until, on
  the last frame, the
  subtitle gives way to the
  finale: "the world is
  yours", one held beat,
  then the fade into the app.
- **The nervous system.**
  The house can be FELT now,
  from any screen. One haptic
  language everywhere: a
  lift-off pattern when a
  process starts, a resolving
  pattern when one lands,
  heavy beats for heavy acts
  (the Banshee, and the
  emergency stop's double
  thud), a soft settle when
  things stand down — wired
  at the organs themselves,
  so a hand, a macro and
  Skelli's own hands all feel
  the same. And the board:
  every process — macros, War
  Room runs, sweeps, port
  scans, the trace, knocks,
  fuzz, watches — registers
  when it starts and reports
  how it landed. A breathing
  strip under the toasts
  counts what is running on
  every tab; one tap opens
  the board itself, with
  elapsed times, a stop for
  anything stoppable, and the
  last dozen endings. Toasts
  grew kinds — news, success,
  warning, error, a process
  reporting in — and repeats
  fold into a count instead
  of stacking. Vanish mutes
  the board too: it files
  everything, and says
  nothing.
- **Vanish — the emergency
  stop.** A mutable button in
  her pinned header: STOP
  while anything runs,
  Vanish when idle. One tap —
  or the words vanish, panic,
  stop everything, go silent —
  halts everything she is
  doing and takes the house
  to full stealth: Serve, the
  beacon and the GATT host
  down, stealth on, EMCON
  dark with the tripwire
  armed. She stays hushed
  until the operator speaks.
- **Stealth is the
  presumption.** She presumes
  stealth is always desired
  unless the operator
  specifically says to be
  loud — in her grounding,
  and in her hands: a Serve
  she raises by voice goes up
  unlisted unless the order
  says loud.
- **Skelli, taught in
  batches.** Fifty-nine new
  manual entries in seven
  batches beyond the rooms:
  her doctrine, the radio and
  wire craft underneath the
  instruments, the rooms
  compared, operations whole,
  failure readings, and a
  communication-patterns set —
  how orders are actually
  phrased, pronouns,
  corrections, negation,
  urgency, quantities, status
  asks, being taught. Lookup,
  her Directory and her mind
  read them automatically.
- **Resume, everywhere.** The
  house reopens exactly where
  it closed: every tab,
  Macros included; the macro
  editor mid-edit, its
  blocks' fold state kept;
  and Skelli's half-written
  line still in her composer.
- **Her macro ears, fixed.**
  A dictated build is no
  longer hijacked by its own
  last verb — she swept
  instead of building, once.
  Natural-speech steps split
  on "and", and she knows
  disguise, mimic and pretend
  for a persona step.
- **The switches.** In
  Colours, under Boot: the
  boot screen itself can be
  switched off — the app
  then opens straight onto
  its tabs — and animated
  GIFs can be stilled
  house-wide: the boot film,
  the wallpaper reel and the
  splashes then stand as
  still frames instead of
  playing.
- **Picture, in two
  modes.** Also under Boot:
  Quality decodes the boot
  film wide, the sphere at
  full density, the reels
  at full size; Performance
  decodes light — about a
  fifth of the film's
  memory, a thinner sphere,
  a capped reel — for hot
  or struggling devices.
- **Your setup, kept.**
  Every setting in the
  house is mirrored into
  the Keychain as it
  changes: the seven-day
  expiry, a deletion and a
  reinstall cannot lose it,
  and a fresh install on
  the same device restores
  it before anything reads
  a setting. About holds
  export and import as one
  file, for a new device;
  a reset forgets the
  mirror too.
- **Provenance, provable.**
  Every build stamps the
  exact commit it was built
  from — and whether the
  tree was clean — into
  the app as it builds.
  About reads it back and,
  on request, holds it
  against the repository's
  current HEAD: matches,
  bit for bit — or exactly
  how it differs.
- **The Vault.** A tab of
  its own: the house's
  cryptography and
  cryptology suite, a
  complete system in
  itself — hashes, HMAC,
  HKDF, PBKDF2, ChaCha20-
  Poly1305 and AES-256-GCM
  sealing, a self-
  describing password
  envelope, P-256 dead-
  drop envelopes, a true
  one-time pad, encodings
  and secure randomness.
  Nine presets ship; the
  operator's own
  configurations save
  beside them and ride
  the setup mirror. Two
  kept keys live in the
  Keychain. Skelli works
  its instant side by
  voice — hashes,
  fingerprints, fresh
  keys and passwords.
- **Her crew.** In
  Skelli's menu, a toggle:
  Agents. On, an ask with
  a plan's shape is
  worked by roles — a
  Planner breaking it
  into steps over the
  house's real resources,
  her own hands working
  them against a shared
  board, a Critic
  checking for gaps, a
  Scribe writing the one
  answer — Qwen or
  Apple's model in
  different states, one
  bench cooperating with
  itself. Runs register
  on the ProcessBoard;
  Vanish stops a crew
  like anything else.
- **The boot, re-voiced.**
  The film is untouched —
  the bird still flies —
  but the text now speaks
  as a key ceremony for a
  crypto release: the
  manifest names its
  Keyholder, its Bedrock,
  its Rendezvous and its
  Witnesses; the Veil is
  drawn or lifted; the
  hour is an Epoch; the
  sphere no longer loads
  the night, it derives
  it; the subtitle stands
  UNDER SEAL until the
  finale breaks it — and
  the manifest seals
  itself with a tenth
  line, the build's own
  provenance.
- **The organized house.**
  About is regrouped —
  Version, Your setup,
  Appearance, This post,
  Listening, The app,
  Defaults, Session logs —
  and explanations across
  About and Colours now
  fold behind a quiet
  "What this does" row:
  terse, exact, and open
  only when asked.
