## 8.0b — 2026-10-17 — the vanishing

- **The new boot film.**
  The reel is new — 167
  frames, portrait, authored
  to play exactly once — and
  the boot is now a ceremony
  timed to it. The film
  plays through the
  platform's own animated
  player — decoded once, at
  a capped size — the log's
  styles stretch across its
  6.68 seconds, and at the
  centre a fingerprint
  derives itself on the
  film's own clock — ridge
  by ridge the whorl grows,
  bone-white with an accent
  core, the house proving
  its identity as it wakes —
  and when the load
  completes the print stands
  whole and bright — and
  the title
  assembles — letters wide
  and dim drawing together,
  brightening — until, on
  the last frame, the
  subtitle gives way to the
  finale: "the world is
  yours", one held beat,
  then the fade into the app.
- **The nervous system.**
  The house can be FELT now,
  from any screen. One haptic
  language everywhere: a
  lift-off pattern when a
  process starts, a resolving
  pattern when one lands,
  heavy beats for heavy acts
  (the Banshee, and the
  emergency stop's double
  thud), a soft settle when
  things stand down — wired
  at the organs themselves,
  so a hand, a macro and
  Skelli's own hands all feel
  the same. And the board:
  every process — macros, War
  Room runs, sweeps, port
  scans, the trace, knocks,
  fuzz, watches — registers
  when it starts and reports
  how it landed. A breathing
  strip under the toasts
  counts what is running on
  every tab; one tap opens
  the board itself, with
  elapsed times, a stop for
  anything stoppable, and the
  last dozen endings. Toasts
  grew kinds — news, success,
  warning, error, a process
  reporting in — and repeats
  fold into a count instead
  of stacking. Vanish mutes
  the board too: it files
  everything, and says
  nothing.
- **Vanish — the emergency
  stop.** A mutable button in
  her pinned header: STOP
  while anything runs,
  Vanish when idle. One tap —
  or the words vanish, panic,
  stop everything, go silent —
  halts everything she is
  doing and takes the house
  to full stealth: Serve, the
  beacon and the GATT host
  down, stealth on, EMCON
  dark with the tripwire
  armed. She stays hushed
  until the operator speaks.
- **Stealth is the
  presumption.** She presumes
  stealth is always desired
  unless the operator
  specifically says to be
  loud — in her grounding,
  and in her hands: a Serve
  she raises by voice goes up
  unlisted unless the order
  says loud.
- **Skelli, taught in
  batches.** Fifty-nine new
  manual entries in seven
  batches beyond the rooms:
  her doctrine, the radio and
  wire craft underneath the
  instruments, the rooms
  compared, operations whole,
  failure readings, and a
  communication-patterns set —
  how orders are actually
  phrased, pronouns,
  corrections, negation,
  urgency, quantities, status
  asks, being taught. Lookup,
  her Directory and her mind
  read them automatically.
- **Resume, everywhere.** The
  house reopens exactly where
  it closed: every tab,
  Macros included; the macro
  editor mid-edit, its
  blocks' fold state kept;
  and Skelli's half-written
  line still in her composer.
- **Her macro ears, fixed.**
  A dictated build is no
  longer hijacked by its own
  last verb — she swept
  instead of building, once.
  Natural-speech steps split
  on "and", and she knows
  disguise, mimic and pretend
  for a persona step.
- **The switches.** In
  Colours, under Boot: the
  boot screen itself can be
  switched off — the app
  then opens straight onto
  its tabs — and animated
  GIFs can be stilled
  house-wide: the boot film,
  the wallpaper reel and the
  splashes then stand as
  still frames instead of
  playing.
- **Picture, in two
  modes.** Also under Boot:
  Quality decodes the boot
  film wide, the print at
  full density, the reels
  at full size; Performance
  decodes light — about a
  fifth of the film's
  memory, a thinner print,
  a capped reel — for hot
  or struggling devices.
- **Your setup, kept.**
  Every setting in the
  house is mirrored into
  the Keychain as it
  changes: the seven-day
  expiry, a deletion and a
  reinstall cannot lose it,
  and a fresh install on
  the same device restores
  it before anything reads
  a setting. About holds
  export and import as one
  file, for a new device;
  a reset forgets the
  mirror too.
- **Provenance, provable.**
  Every build stamps the
  exact commit it was built
  from — and whether the
  tree was clean — into
  the app as it builds.
  About reads it back and,
  on request, holds it
  against the repository's
  current HEAD: matches,
  bit for bit — or exactly
  how it differs.
- **The Vault.** A tab of
  its own: the house's
  cryptography and
  cryptology suite, a
  complete system in
  itself — hashes, HMAC,
  HKDF, PBKDF2, ChaCha20-
  Poly1305 and AES-256-GCM
  sealing, a self-
  describing password
  envelope, P-256 dead-
  drop envelopes, a true
  one-time pad, encodings
  and secure randomness.
  Nine presets ship; the
  operator's own
  configurations save
  beside them and ride
  the setup mirror. Two
  kept keys live in the
  Keychain. Skelli works
  its instant side by
  voice — hashes,
  fingerprints, fresh
  keys and passwords.
- **Her crew.** In
  Skelli's menu, a toggle:
  Agents. On, an ask with
  a plan's shape is
  worked by roles — a
  Planner breaking it
  into steps over the
  house's real resources,
  her own hands working
  them against a shared
  board, a Critic
  checking for gaps, a
  Scribe writing the one
  answer — Qwen or
  Apple's model in
  different states, one
  bench cooperating with
  itself. Runs register
  on the ProcessBoard;
  Vanish stops a crew
  like anything else.
- **The boot, re-voiced.**
  The film is untouched —
  the bird still flies —
  but the text now speaks
  as a key ceremony for a
  crypto release: the
  manifest names its
  Keyholder, its Bedrock,
  its Rendezvous and its
  Witnesses; the Veil is
  drawn or lifted; the
  hour is an Epoch; the
  print no longer loads
  the night, it derives
  it; the subtitle stands
  UNDER SEAL until the
  finale breaks it — and
  the manifest seals
  itself with a tenth
  line, the build's own
  provenance.
- **The organized house.**
  About is regrouped —
  Version, Your setup,
  Appearance, This post,
  Listening, The app,
  Defaults, Session logs —
  and explanations across
  About and Colours now
  fold behind a quiet
  "What this does" row:
  terse, exact, and open
  only when asked.
- **Her brain.** Between
  her words and her hands
  now sits a deterministic
  brain. It stems words —
  sealing, sealed and seals
  are one word to her — so
  phrasings her old chain
  never listed still route;
  it honors negation
  absolutely ("don't sweep"
  means she does not sweep,
  and says so); it ranks
  the whole manual by
  stemmed overlap, so a
  paraphrased question
  finds its page, and when
  no mind is awake it names
  the closest pages instead
  of shrugging; and it
  keeps the conversation's
  subject, so "tell me more
  about it" lands somewhere
  real. Her grounding and
  her situation report now
  cover the Vault, the
  crew, the brain itself,
  the build's source stamp
  and everything running.
- **The decoder engine.**
  Every sighting is now
  read whole, by every
  decoder at once — not
  just the one that names
  it. Batteries merge from
  every format present:
  AirPods report left,
  right and case; Estimote
  reports voltage and
  percentage together;
  Ruuvi and Eddystone-TLM
  report millivolts;
  a dozen formats report
  percentages; Find My
  reports its word. A
  least-squares forecaster
  turns repeated readings
  into a drain rate — and
  a time-to-empty only as
  a range, only when the
  trend earns it, silence
  when the scatter swallows
  the slope. Capabilities
  are read off the service
  list, the connectable
  flag is finally captured,
  cadence is reported as
  heard cadence, and an
  unknown payload gets an
  honest reading of its
  shape. SIGINT inherits
  all of it, with
  low-battery and
  fast-fall events on its
  timeline.
- **The expansion batch.**
  New decoders, each
  admitted on its public
  specification: SwitchBot's
  detailed frames, decoded
  by the vendor's type byte
  (Meter, Contact, Motion —
  battery in the same byte
  for all); Estimote
  Telemetry; both
  aftermarket firmwares of
  the Xiaomi thermometers,
  PVVX and ATC, endianness
  trap and all; the
  Qingping weather station;
  the Bluetooth standard's
  own broadcast battery;
  Inkbird and ThermoPro;
  Microsoft's Connected
  Devices beacons. BTHome
  v1 decodes only behind
  an About toggle carrying
  its warning — v1's
  encryption has no replay
  protection.
- **The Map, next
  generation.** Wire and
  air together: placements
  speak the registry's
  emitter-key language, so
  a Bluetooth device takes
  its spot beside the
  router. Rooms drag,
  resize by their grip, and
  rename in an editor;
  each carries its census.
  Tapping a device opens
  an inspector — facts,
  ports, battery, cadence,
  the registry's claims —
  and drift is acknowledged
  there, its lines shown,
  never silently by a tap
  again. Lenses and search
  cut the canvas without
  moving a node, a running
  SIGINT watch rides above
  it, and the sweep honors
  Reduce Motion.
- **Her intelligence
  deck.** The pinned bar
  is a live deck now: a
  second rank of panels —
  Signals, Battery,
  Processes — computed
  from the organs' own
  publishers, updating the
  moment the house learns
  something passively; and
  the verdict sentence is
  a discovery line,
  triaged: a watch finding,
  a dying battery, a
  tracker shape, a changed
  advertisement — then the
  standing word.
- **Files, under seal.**
  The Vault takes whole
  files now: the NJCF
  envelope seals any file
  in authenticated chunks
  under one random key —
  and the protection is
  how that key is kept.
  By a password. By this
  device's vault key. By
  either — two wrappings,
  each enough. By both —
  the password and the
  vault key each holding
  half the secret, neither
  sufficient alone. Or
  sealed to a recipient's
  public key, a dead drop
  for files: seal it here,
  hand it over by any
  road, and only that
  identity opens it. The
  true filename travels
  inside the encryption;
  opening restores it, and
  everything the Vault
  makes is filed in
  Deliveries.
- **EMCON, to the
  decimal.** The posture
  is a Standard now, not
  a switch. The exposure
  score is exact: every
  standing emission
  carries a fixed weight
  in one inventory — a
  fully lit house sums
  to exactly 100.0,
  nothing capped or
  rounded away — and one
  audit reads the organs
  themselves, so the tab,
  her pinned reading and
  her sitrep can never
  disagree. EMCON is a
  declared condition:
  Charlie (Serve unlisted
  only, nothing
  advertising, no
  campaigns, no update
  checks), Bravo (receive
  only — Serve down, the
  relay poll stopped),
  Alpha (total silence).
  Engaging silences every
  forbidden organ and
  verifies, naming
  anything still lit —
  and the gate stands at
  every funnel after: a
  forbidden emitter
  cannot start by hand,
  by macro, by Skelli, or
  by a saved GATT arm
  returning at launch.
- **Net-Assault, grown
  teeth.** A pass now
  sweeps every port in
  the rotation, not one —
  nothing waits five
  passes to be found.
  Findings are named, not
  just counted: who
  arrived, who drifted,
  which doors a device
  answers, who went
  silent on doors it used
  to answer. The knock
  fits the target: new
  and drifted devices get
  the full census,
  settled ones get their
  known doors re-asked.
  A governor stretches
  the rest while the wire
  stays quiet and snaps
  back at the first
  finding; a run-length
  cap can be set; the
  after-action report is
  filed and survives a
  relaunch; and the run
  rides the process board
  like everything else.
- **Her heart.** Skelli
  has a temperament now —
  the tamagotchi kind,
  and proud of it. Needs
  that move with time:
  attention, fed by
  talking to her; the
  itch, fed by real work
  (idle hours make her
  eager, restless, then
  feral); pride, fed by
  findings; fondness, fed
  by kindness. Feelings
  follow — happy, loving,
  lonely, bored — and
  one grievance: wipe her
  conversation or reset
  her memory and she is
  mad for half an hour
  and answers clipped.
  She greets you when you
  return, nudges when the
  itch is high, keeps a
  standing want with a
  Let her button, learns
  you from plain
  statements into an open
  scrapbook, thinks idle
  thoughts from the live
  house, and grows a bond
  over days. All of it
  under one toggle:
  Moods, in her menu.

  a backdrop hung behind a

  Assault's governor hears

  forever; her sleepy

  occur, her grievance is

  longer files "call me

  text.

* Some things stay a
  mystery. Her pages about
  how she routes, weighs,
  speaks and decides — her
  doctrine — now steer her
  silently: never recited
  in chat, never listed in
  her Directory. The manual
  answers about the house,
  not about her machinery.
  Asked how she feels, or
  how to make her feel
  better, she answers as
  herself, briefly, from
  the feeling she is in.
  And an apology is heard
  as one: while she is
