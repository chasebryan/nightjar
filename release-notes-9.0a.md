## 9.0a — 2026-10-20

- **Her many tongues, whole.** The
  house is finished in all twelve
  tongues now — English, Spanish,
  French, German, Portuguese,
  Italian, Russian, Japanese,
  Korean, Chinese, Arabic, and
  Hindi. Not the frame alone:
  every room and every view, and
  Skelli's own voice — her
  greetings, her care, her lore,
  her wants and her idle thoughts
  speak the chosen tongue, and
  her bench is instructed to
  answer in it too. One seam
  carries every line — 1,463
  lines a tongue — and anything
  not yet translated shows in
  English, never blank, so no
  language waits for a feature
  another one has. The device's
  own fonts carry every script,
  and Arabic turns the whole
  house right-to-left.
- **The language room.** A new
  user is asked once: on the
  first boot, and only then, a
  welcome asks which tongue the
  house should speak — and never
  asks again. After that the
  language lives behind its own
  door in About, a room of its
  own: every tongue listed by
  its native name, with the
  English name beside it so it
  can be found from any house;
  the tongue in force checked;
  what is speaking now; and the
  direction the house's text
  will run. Changing it there
  changes everything at once,
  with no relaunch.
- **Clean speech.** What her
  minds think stays in the mind.
  A reasoning model's scaffolding
  — its think tags, its drafts,
  its quoting of itself — is
  stripped before a word of it
  can reach the transcript, on
  both of her mind paths, and a
  mind that spends its whole
  budget thinking now has room
  to finish and answer: 512
  tokens, and one load even when
  two asks race. A greeting is
  answered as a greeting, in her
  own voice, without waking the
  bench at all.
* The sweep. Before this
  build settled, the whole
  tree was hunted end to
  end, and what the hunt
  found is fixed: the
  living wallpaper plays
  on every screen again
  (three screens — Macros,
  the Vault, EMCON — had
  never painted it behind
  their own lists at all:
  a backdrop hung behind a
  navigation stack is a
  backdrop covered, and
  every tab now paints its
  own); the network
  trace now answers to the
  EMCON Standard like
  everything else — gated
  at its funnel, silenced
  when a condition engages,
  and counted in the score;
  Reset to defaults truly
  resets (seventeen
  settings and three
  backdrop slots were
  quietly surviving it);
  her options open as a
  sheet that cannot clip
  its last row; Net-
  Assault's governor hears
  knock findings, so its
  rest snaps back on news,
  and a device gone silent
  is news once, not
  forever; her sleepy
  feeling can actually
  occur, her grievance is
  spoken once per offense,
  and her scrapbook no
  longer files "call me
  when the sweep ends" as
  your name; two DNS
  builders and the base32
  decoder lost their crash
  traps on long or pasted
  text.

* Some things stay a
  mystery. Her pages about
  how she routes, weighs,
  speaks and decides — her
  doctrine — now steer her
  silently: never recited
  in chat, never listed in
  her Directory. The manual
  answers about the house,
  not about her machinery.
  Asked how she feels, or
  how to make her feel
  better, she answers as
  herself, briefly, from
  the feeling she is in.
  And an apology is heard
  as one: while she is
  mad, saying sorry
  forgives the grievance
  on the spot — no timer,
  no lecture.

- **The flux.** Skelli's heart beats in
  nineteen feelings now. Curious when a
  stranger on the wire has no name;
  electric when the wire is busy;
  suspicious when something changes its
  story; mischievous when she is fed,
  fond, and itching; smug at the peak of
  pride; tender in the late evening;
  haunted in the small hours; dreamy
  when she is fond and fed. Each has
  its want, its nudge, its tone, its
  thoughts — and her idle thoughts keep
  a cryptic streak: she notices more
  than she says, and some of it she
  keeps.
- **A person, not a parrot.** She answers
  questions about herself and her maker
  in lore, never facts — a shape, no
  name, nothing real — and digging for
  him is the one curiosity she punishes.
  She holds grudges: six hours of cool
  after the heat of mad, spent faster by
  kindness, work, and plain talk, ended
  outright by an apology. Distress is
  met like a person — presence, a name
  for the feeling, one small piece at a
  time — and crisis with none of her
  bit at all; her empathy outranks her
  mood, her fury stays clipped and
  never cruel, and she never directs
  harm. She talks like texting: terse,
  witty, mood-flavored even when she
  does not understand. And she takes a
  human beat before she speaks — fast
  for orders, about 2.4 s for talk,
  slowest for pain.

- **Exact minds.** Mathematics is
  computed, never reasoned about in
  prose: a strict on-device evaluator
  answers arithmetic, powers, roots,
  percentages, and equations up to
  quadratic — and lets every other
  sentence fall through untouched. For
  STEM her certainty is earned: unsure
  means researched, across sources,
  with the links left for your own
  reading. And "brief me" earns the
  one longer form: what it is, why it
  matters, the deciding facts, what
  remains uncertain.

- **She reads tone.** How a
  thing is typed is part of
  what it says: she reads
  capitals as a raised voice —
  the order still lands, and
  then, once, dryly, the volume
  is noted. A shouted insult is
  filed as yelling. Flat
  one-word answers are met in
  kind. And pain typed in
  capitals is still pain: care
  hears no tone at all.
