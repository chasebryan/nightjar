## 7.0.0 — 2026-10-17 — the war chest

- **The War Chest.** Whole-rig
  mission profiles: one chest
  captures the standing
  configuration of Send, Serve,
  Recon and the trace — every
  setting, including stealth —
  and applies it in one tap.
  Applying a chest dresses the
  rig; it starts nothing. The
  old presets migrate once,
  untouched, and chests share
  as JSON.
- **The Helm.** Named remotes
  for your own gear: big buttons
  that fire the payload book
  through the Send path — TCP,
  UDP, HTTP, or another phone —
  built from the book, the Send
  composer, or Learn mode, which
  binds what your gear sends you
  into a button. Beside it in
  EMCON, the tag census: your
  own NFC cards and tags read
  whole — NDEF, the chip's own
  identity, and an APDU explorer
  for ISO 7816. Nothing is
  written; nothing is emulated.
- **The War Room.** Listen, the
  trace and the knock bench run
  as one operation on one named
  /24, feeding one timestamped
  timeline — sightings, hosts,
  doors, banner changes, knock
  verdicts — and one export
  carries the whole record, the
  trace's own export embedded
  whole.
- **The SIGINT Station.**
  Everything the phone can hear,
  fused into one emitter
  registry: BLE hearing, the LAN
  census, Night-log activity.
  Records merge only within an
  epoch — no cross-epoch
  stitching, ever. Watches
  record a named span and file
  themselves with their delta:
  what arrived, what went
  silent, what changed. The
  station's limits are stated
  in its own interface and in
  every export it writes.
- **War Games.** Two of your
  phones spar: one defends —
  Serve up, decoys flying — the
  other attacks, trace and
  knock. The match protocol
  rides the Serve inbox itself:
  challenge, ready, start,
  armed, done, cards. The
  scoring diffs found against
  missed against caught against
  decoys, and writes the match
  report.
- **The Banshee.** EMCON's
  opposite pole, one switch in
  the posture tab: Serve listed
  with stealth off, the BLE
  beacon shouting BANSHEE,
  Net-Assault armed, and a
  named broadcast heartbeat
  across the subnet — this
  device announcing itself at
  full voice until you cease
  it. Ceasing restores the
  exact posture the scream
  found. It announces only
  itself; it touches no one
  else's traffic.
- **The exposure score,**
  **honestly based.** EMCON's
  number now starts from
  presence: a phone joined to a
  wire, answering under its own
  name, was never a ghost.
  Being on the wire, and the
  name it answers to, are
  weighed first — then the live
  emissions. The itemised parts
  under the score say which is
  which.
