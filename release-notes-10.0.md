# nightjar-hack 10.0 — release notes

The chorus release: every voice in the house, singing at once.

## 10.0 — 2026-10-20 — chorus
- **Chorus opens.** The tenth version of the house begins: the console below is an operating system, Skelli is awake inside it, the game room plays, and every instrument the place owns sounds at once. What chorus comes to mean will be written here as it lands.
- **Skelli, relearned.** Her hands answer to verbs now, not nouns: an ask she cannot act on is no longer answered with her one canned trick — the canary rises only when raising is what was asked, and she can knock her own decoy like a stranger and read back what it filed. Chained orders run in order — 'raise the ssh canary, then knock the canary' does both — and she reads her own inbox aloud: the newest arrival, whichever road it came by, and what it said. Questions about her instruments get her knowledge; orders get her hands.

- **Skelli, in variation.** No line of hers is the only way she can say it any more. A memory now rides every pool she speaks from, so she never hands back the line she just gave; the pools themselves run deep — greetings, thanks, laughter, agreement, her working words, the frames around her reports — and the moment steers the draw: her feeling, the hour, the season, the moon, EMCON's condition, the conductor's wakefulness, how long she has kept this house. Her idle thoughts keep a longer cryptic streak, her circumstances speak in more phrasings, and all of it holds in eleven languages.
- **A twelfth tongue: Schwiizerdütsch.** The house learns Anna Murphy's native language — Swiss German, written the way it is written, Lucerne-leaning: a full table beside the other eleven, chosen in the language room like any other. Ech lose zue.
- **Every signal, read — and the router, known by signs.** The decoders grow the formats that exist (MikroTik's TG-BT5 tag, decoded whole from the vendor's own published layout), Bonjour TXT records are read at last (an AirPlay target's model, device id and capability bits; a printer's stated location), and where no format exists the house reasons from evidence instead: the default gateway is crowned the router by definition, every other neighbor is weighed by its signs — the doors it holds, the chair it sits at, the name it answers to, MikroTik's own introductions over MNDP — with the evidence shown and the language never outrunning it. On the air, long dwell, an unmoving signal and a metronome cadence testify to fixed infrastructure. Ask the console: `router`.
- **Administer your own gear.** The console grows a credentialed SNMP hand: `cred set` keeps a community for your own network in the Keychain (typed in secret — echoed as bullets, never written in a command line or the history), `snmpwalk` walks the MIB with it, and `snmpset` writes one object and reads it straight back to show what landed.
