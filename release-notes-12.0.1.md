# nightjar 12B — release notes

The plumage fix release: the crash convicted and killed, TOTALITY restructured on principle, and the console's python made whole.

## 12.0.1 — 2026-10-23 — plumage (12B)
- **The crash is dead.** The payload beacon's crash — seven records, one family — was convicted at last: an iOS peripheral may advertise its name and its service UUIDs, and nothing else; payload bytes in service data were never legal, in any shape. Beacons now advertise name and service alone, and a payload that cannot legally ride is set down and said so plainly in the log instead of being forced onto the air. The ladder of shapes is retired; the lesson stays in the code where the next reader will find it.
- **TOTALITY, restructured on principle.** The house keeps three kinds of command — magdump the offensive one, eazy the passive dumper, TOTALITY the raising of the house itself — and TOTALITY no longer blurs them: it raises no beacon and runs no assault. It stands the house up (her door served under EMCON's say-so, the house tap running, ears always open) and reports what it raised; the same word backwards stands down only that.
- **Skelli runs it.** Asked in chat, she raises TOTALITY, stands it down, and reads its state back in her own voice — the console's word in her hands, like every other instrument in the house.
- **magdump takes a plug.** `magdump <host> <payload>` fires one named payload from the book at the doors the dump found open — the offensive sibling made pluggable, under the house's one-knock honesty. A door that speaks TLS is named, never fired at bare.
- **Python, whole.** The console's python learned to fetch and run real programs end to end. `load owner/repo` lands a GitHub program and runs its front door with the repo as its working folder; `pip install` settles pure-python packages and their dependencies from PyPI into /packages — a compiled or source-only package is named as unrunnable, never faked. Imports take packages, dotted paths, and relatives. The language itself grew decorators, annotations, `*args` and `**kwargs` on both sides of a call, property, staticmethod and classmethod, and `sys.exit` with real stdout and stderr. And a twelve-module stdlib shelf — typing, enum, dataclasses, pathlib, argparse, csv, glob, fnmatch, textwrap, logging, shutil, contextlib — each module carrying its honest edges in its own header.
- **The Mac compiles again.** One argument stood out of order in the Mac's film player; both platforms declare it the same way now, and the whole house builds on both kinds of glass.
